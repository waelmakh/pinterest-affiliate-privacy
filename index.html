Privacy Policy

Pin Affiliate Poster · Last updated 21 August 2026

Overview

Pin Affiliate Poster ("the App") is a personal automation tool that publishes product Pins to Pinterest boards owned by its operator. This policy explains what data the App handles and how it is used. The App is operated by an individual, not a company.

Information the App accesses

The App connects to Pinterest through the official Pinterest API using OAuth 2.0. With the permission you grant during sign-in, it may access:

  • Your Pinterest account identifier and public profile details
  • Your boards and the Pins on them
  • The ability to create Pins on boards you own
  • Basic analytics about Pins the App has created

The App requests only the permission scopes it needs for these functions. It never asks for your Pinterest password, and it cannot see it.

How the information is used

The data above is used solely to:

  • Publish Pins to boards the operator controls
  • Avoid creating duplicate Pins
  • Measure how published Pins perform

The App does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and retention

Access tokens and Pin records are stored privately on the operator's own machine or private server. They are not published, shared, or uploaded to any third-party service. Data is deleted when it is no longer needed, or immediately on request.

Sharing with third parties

The App does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. It communicates only with the Pinterest API and with the affiliate program platforms whose product catalogues it reads. No Pinterest account data is sent to those platforms.

Affiliate disclosure

Pins created by the App may contain affiliate links. If someone makes a purchase through one of those links, the operator may earn a commission at no extra cost to the buyer. Affiliate Pins are labelled as such in line with Pinterest's community guidelines and applicable advertising disclosure rules.

Cookies and tracking

This page sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. The App itself has no web interface that visitors can browse.

Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Pinterest account settings, under Settings → Security and permissions → Apps. Revoking access immediately stops all further data access. You may also request deletion of any stored data using the contact address below.

Children

The App is not directed at anyone under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
